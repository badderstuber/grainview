Multigrain "Screen" module — OLED options

Pixel-accurate. Each is drawn at native resolution, quantized to the panel's grey depth, then scaled up crisp — so this is genuinely how the OLED would look.

A · 1-bit mono

SSD1306 / SH1106 — 128×64, 0.96–1.3"
~6–8 HP · exact Digitakt crispness
Razor-sharp, no shading. Waveform fills solid white — pixel-art look. Cheapest, most "classic Elektron".

B · 16-level greyscale

SSD1327 — 128×128, 1.5" square
~8–10 HP · compact + shading
Grey levels give soft waveform fill + anti-aliased text. Square format fits more rows. My recommendation for a compact module.

C · 16-level greyscale

SSD1322 — 256×64, 3.12" wide
~14–18 HP · the showpiece
The boutique-synth wide display. Cinematic waveform, gorgeous — but eats rack space.
Representative "SOUND 1" view: title + waveform (START/WRAP markers, SCAN playhead) + live params. Greys quantized to the real panel depth.
